## Overview

PipeWire is designed to replace PulseAudio and JACK. It provides low latency processing for pro-audio while supporting desktop needs.

## Why use it?
- Low latency Bluetooth support (LDAC, aptX)
- Seamless Wayland screen sharing via WebRTC / xdg-desktop-portal
